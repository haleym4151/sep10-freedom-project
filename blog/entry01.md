# EDP
 EDP, which stands for Engineering Design Process has 8 steps: Define problems, Research the problem, Brainstorm solutions, Plan a promising solution, Create prototypes, Test and evaluate the prototype, Improve the solution, and lastly, Communicate the results. Right now I am on the first step of my EDP, defining the problem with Arts & Crafts & Design, which is a color picker for traditional artists. A device that can copy any color you place the device on. Afterwards, I'll move onto step two of EDP, "Research the problem".
